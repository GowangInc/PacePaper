## Scope

This snapshot covers every course currently exposed by the DigitalDP Paper Builder:

- English A: Language and Literature, English A: Literature, and English B
- Korean A: Language and Literature and Korean A: Literature
- Japanese A: Language and Literature and Japanese A: Literature
- Spanish A: Language and Literature, Spanish A: Literature, and Spanish B
- Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches and Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation
- Biology, Chemistry, and Physics
- Business Management and Psychology

The collectors retained every matching paper, markscheme, booklet, insert, pre-seen or pre-release file, transcript, associated support file, and Language B listening recording exposed by the mirror branches that were crawled.

Their local sizes match the source `Content-Length`, so neither is a truncated local download. The originals and hashes were retained without rewriting them.

## 2026 availability boundary

The mirror exposed only a partial **More Papers M26** branch when collected. It did not expose complete May 2026 or November 2026 session trees. The archive contains the 2026 items that were actually listed, but it must not be read as proof that other 2026 papers or materials do not exist. Each detailed provenance report and availability matrix records this limitation.
