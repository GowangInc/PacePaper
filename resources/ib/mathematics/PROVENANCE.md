## Known gaps and legitimate access routes

- The downloaded public full guides are older revisions. The authoritative current versions are **AA: February 2019, updated November 2024** and **AI: February 2019, updated August 2025**, according to the retained official examiner instructions. Authorized school staff should obtain them from the [Programme Resource Centre](https://resources.ibo.org/); purchased material is available through the IB/Follett routes described on the [IB educational-resources page](https://www.ibo.org/become-an-ib-school/ib-publishing/educational-resources-for-schools/).
- No full first-assessment-2029 mathematics guide or official 2029 specimen pack was publicly available on 2026-09-01. The IB says the revised AA course launches in February 2027; use the briefs only for advance planning and refresh after launch.
- The newest freely public mathematics examples remain the first-assessment-2021 specimen packs on the IB sample-paper page, which was last updated in February 2026.
- Actual past papers and markschemes are licensed materials. Legitimate routes are the [IB Questionbank](https://questionbank.ibo.org/) and the IB/Follett store. Do not source them from mirrors or add them to a distributable repository.
- Apply the [IB intellectual-property terms](https://www.ibo.org/terms-and-conditions/intellectual-property/) before reproducing any IB question, markscheme, examination stationery, or purchased material.
