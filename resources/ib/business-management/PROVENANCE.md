## Public examples and the specimen-paper gap

- The public [Business management subject page](https://www.ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/individuals-and-societies/business-and-management/) says it was updated 6 March 2026, but its assessment summary still describes the superseded model (two written papers at both levels and different SL/HL internal assessments). It conflicts with the first-assessment-2024 guide, the 2026 examiner instructions, and the 2026 schedules, so it was not used to populate presets.
- The official [sample examination papers](https://www.ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/assessment-and-exams/sample-exam-papers/) landing page was checked on 2026-09-01. No Business Management specimen/question-paper pack was publicly listed.
- Two current-cycle May 2025 markschemes are publicly indexed on the IB domain: [HL/SL Paper 1](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides-documents/sl-and-hl-mark-scheme-paper-1--business-management.pdf) and [SL Paper 2](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides-documents/sl-mark-scheme-paper-2--business-management.pdf). They are markschemes, not specimen or question papers, and were not needed or retained for the structural findings.
- The guide itself directs teachers to the authenticated [Programme Resource Centre](https://resources.ibo.org/) for specimen papers, markschemes, teacher support materials, subject reports, and grade descriptors, and to the IB store for past papers and markschemes.
- The licensed [IB Questionbank](https://questionbank.ibo.org/) lists DP Business Management and supports custom examinations using official questions. This is the legitimate route for an official-question builder; DigitalDP should not reproduce that library.
- The IB’s [educational resources for schools](https://www.ibo.org/become-an-ib-school/ib-publishing/educational-resources-for-schools/) page describes authorized purchasing routes. Apply the [IB intellectual-property terms](https://www.ibo.org/terms-and-conditions/intellectual-property/) before importing, displaying, or redistributing any official questions, papers, markschemes, or formula sheets.
