## Shared official controls used but not duplicated here

- The official [May 2026 DP/CP examination schedule](../mathematics/PROVENANCE.md#may-2026-dp-and-cp-examination-schedule) verifies current paper durations and shows Language B reading and listening as separately scheduled components.
- The current public [Access and inclusion policy](../policies/SOURCES.md#access-and-inclusion-policy) establishes a five-minute reading period before written/on-screen examinations. It does not state the candidate interaction rules during those five minutes; session-specific conduct instructions in the PRC remain necessary.
- The Language B public specimen supplies a more specific listening script: four minutes to read at the start of each audio text, answers permitted at any time, each audio played twice, and a two-minute pause before the repeat. Whether any additional general five-minute period applies to the listening component was not verified publicly and must not be inferred.

## Known gaps and legitimate access routes

- The full current Language A: literature and Language A: language and literature guides for **first assessment 2026** were not publicly downloadable from official IB pages. Authorized school staff should obtain them from the [Programme Resource Centre](https://resources.ibo.org/).
- No recent official public Korean A, Japanese A, Spanish A, Spanish B, or Language A: language and literature specimen/past-paper files were found on the IB public sample-paper page. Use the PRC, the school's IB coordinator, or licensed [IB Questionbank](https://questionbank.ibo.org/) access. Absence from the public page is not evidence that those materials do not exist in licensed channels.
- The school's exact language-course offering is unknown. The official 2026 subject list verifies availability, not local enrollment or authorization.
- Current public sources reviewed did not identify a fixed separate preparation period for the Language A individual oral. Do not invent one; confirm the current guide and assessment procedures in the PRC.
- Do not use `ibdocs`, file-sharing mirrors, or other unauthorized repositories. Do not add licensed/private past papers to Git. Apply the [IB intellectual-property terms](https://www.ibo.org/terms-and-conditions/intellectual-property/) before reproducing any IB content.
