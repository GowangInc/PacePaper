## Important 2026 limitation

The 2026 index exposed only **More Papers M26** when retrieved. It did not expose complete May 2026 or November 2026 session trees. The six retained 2026 files are therefore a **partial mirror snapshot**, not a claim that other 2026 course materials do not exist. The detailed availability CSV marks every 2026 course row accordingly.

## Acquisition and verification

- The collector crawled each year index, its listed May/November/More Papers session areas, relevant language directories, and document landing pages.
- Actual English B and Spanish B recordings use opaque subject codes. Audio links whose filenames contain `abeng` or `abspa` were retained; `aneng` and `anspa` (language ab initio) were excluded.
- Archive-relative directories and source filenames were preserved.
- Every retained binary was checked against its recorded byte size and recomputed SHA-256 digest after download.
- Per-year manifests and checksum files, plus the combined private manifest and checksum file, live beside the ignored binaries.
- `past-papers-2022-2026-inventory.csv` is the tracked row-level provenance index. `past-papers-2022-2026-availability.csv` explicitly records present, absent, and partial coverage for every requested year/course combination.
