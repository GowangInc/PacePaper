The [IB Questionbank](https://questionbank.ibo.org/) is the legitimate licensed route for building custom tests from official questions and lists the current Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Psychology courses.

## Cross-subject controls

- The current public *Access and inclusion policy* is retained once in [`../policies/`](../policies/SOURCES.md). Section 4.12 establishes a five-minute reading period before written/on-screen examinations and says authorized additional time does not extend it.
- The [2026 DP assessment procedures](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/become-an-ib-school/pdfs/dp-2026-general-regulations.pdf) make the annual *Conduct of examinations booklet 2026* the operational authority for running examinations. The public policy does not establish whether candidates may annotate or otherwise interact during the five-minute phase; confirm that detail in the current PRC conduct booklet.
- The [exam calculator policy](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/assessment-and-exams/exam-calculator-policy/) routes approved-model details to the PRC. The subject guides establish whether a calculator is permitted/required, but the permitted device list remains session-specific.
- The [digital examinations page](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/assessment-and-exams/digital-examinations-for-the-dp-and-cp/) says digital exams initially mirror paper assessments and that live digital specimens are exclusive to IB World Schools through the PRC. Public sources do not expose the permitted subject-specific input tools.
