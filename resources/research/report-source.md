# DigitalDP exam-format research record

Audience: DigitalDP maintainers and subject teachers\
Current review date: 5 September 2026\
Decision: which timings, mark totals, choices, resources and response controls may be presented as current exam-format presets and full-format original practice papers.

## Scope and standard

This record covers the IB Diploma Programme courses presently exposed by the Paper Builder. It separates the current 2026 assessment cycle from announced future cycles. It does not authorize reproduction of official questions, passages, diagrams, markschemes or stationery. All DigitalDP demo content must be independently written.

A demo may be called **full-format practice** only when all of the following are true:

- its level, component, duration, mark total, compulsory/choice structure and permitted materials match a current primary source;
- every mark is represented by an original question or explicit subpart in the DigitalDP manifest;
- source material is long and varied enough for the component rather than a token paragraph;
- the student view exposes all material that the real component permits candidates to inspect;
- the application has a matching automated format assertion and a completed candidate-flow test.

Anything short of that standard must be labelled **compact walkthrough**, **format rehearsal**, or **awaiting subject-teacher validation**. “Original” describes authorship, not official endorsement.

The bundled library currently contains 46 independently authored examples. Of its 34 IB-oriented papers, 20 are validated in code as **original full-format practice** across English A, English B, Mathematics AA, Mathematics AI, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Psychology and Business Management. The remaining Korean, Japanese and Spanish papers are explicitly presented as format rehearsals rather than as validated full-format facsimiles. The twelve Cambridge, Pearson and AP examples remain compact samples or accelerated walkthroughs unless their titles explicitly say otherwise.

## Rights boundary and stopping rule

The official guides and public papers are format evidence only. The IB source files remain in `resources/` and are excluded from Git. The distributable app contains independently written practice material, not copied questions, passages, diagrams or marks schemes. Research for a component stops when its consequential format fields have primary support, contradictions are documented, the generated manifest satisfies those fields, and the candidate/teacher workflows pass. It resumes when the IB publishes a new guide or session procedure, or when an authorized teacher supplies a current PRC control document.
