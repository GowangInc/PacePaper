### Calculus navigation and administrative transitions

DigitalDP locks expired parts, including II-A when II-B begins. Delivery matters when comparing this with official rules: the [2025–26 accommodated-paper script, pp.2 and 10](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-calculus-ab-bc-paper-exam-instructions.pdf) permits work on II-A during II-B without a calculator, but explicitly excludes digital administrations from that script's scope. The [2026 hybrid FRQ directions, pp.2 and 6](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap26-frq-calculus-ab.pdf) describe moving among questions within the current part and do not state that earlier-part permission. The current 2027 CED does not resolve this navigation distinction. Therefore, the mock discloses its stricter-than-accommodated-paper behavior without claiming that Bluebook currently permits the same return. Check the final 2027 delivery-specific directions before treating the mock as an administration simulation.

The [2025–26 coordinator manual, printed p.80](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-coordinators-manual-part-2.pdf) documents a one-minute transition between A and B in both digital Calculus sections. This mock's 200-minute elapsed schedule omits those two administrative transitions while preserving all 190 minutes of assessed working time and its ten-minute break. The transitions are not extra working time. Both the student instructions and teacher companion disclose the omission.

## Teacher marking, weighting and safeguards

Every MCQ has a zero-based correct option and an explanation. Every FRQ has explicit whole-point allocations and worked answers. This companion information is held in `FullMock.marking` and is **not** added to a candidate manifest or resource. The integrating teacher-only guide must preserve that boundary.

- Biology practice composite: `50 × MCQ/60 + 50 × FRQ/34`.
- Calculus practice composite: `35 × MCQ-A/29 + 15 × MCQ-B/13 + 50 × FRQ/54`. The current CED specifies 35%/15% for the two MCQ parts; an equal raw weighting of all 42 MCQ is not exactly that allocation.
- Neither the 94-point Biology raw percentage nor the 96-point Calculus raw percentage is the correct weighted composite. No AP 1–5 conversion is provided.
- These are internally reviewed complete workloads, **not externally moderated, equated or calibrated against real AP score boundaries**. A qualified subject teacher should review demand and marking before consequential grading.
- Tables, ASCII pathway/tree/food-web diagrams, and exact graph-coordinate descriptions replace some production graphics. Candidates draw required graphs in the integrated grid. This is an explicit digital-visual adaptation, not a claim to reproduce every visual feature of Bluebook.

### Independent Biology semantic review and repair

A second author checked MCQs **1, 4, 8, 12, 15, 20, 25, 31, 39, 46, 55 and 60**, covering all eight units, and all six FRQ prompts, supplied prerequisites and marking schemes. The selected MCQ keys and FRQs 1–5 were consistent. This is a targeted internal review, not a complete independent audit of all 60 MCQs or external teacher moderation.

The review found that FRQ 6 originally specified fixed cohorts of 100 beetles of each color while using only surviving counts in a goodness-of-fit test. That cohort design would require a 2×2 survival/death comparison: its four-cell Pearson statistic would be 25.92, not 12.96. A homogeneity-of-proportions contingency test sums all cells, not just survivors. [NIST statistical handbook: comparing proportions](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc46.htm).

The repaired question instead specifies a simple random sample of 100 survivors from a very large population with known initial 50:50 color composition; the sample is a negligible population fraction. Its stated null is unchanged survivor composition, so expected sample counts are 50 and 50 and the intended two-category goodness-of-fit statistic is **12.96**. Resource, prompt and key now distinguish sample composition from unmeasured color-specific survival rates. Evolutionary inference remains explicitly conditional on consistent differential survival, inheritance and reproductive success. The question retains four marks.

MCQ 39's best experiment now uses purified receptor and isolated DNA with specificity/background controls, avoiding ambiguity between direct DNA binding and association bridged by other cellular proteins. Focused regression checks cover both repairs in addition to the existing structural and numerical suite.
