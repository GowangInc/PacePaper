# IB DP sciences and psychology assessment and Paper Builder findings

Research snapshot: **2026-09-01**
Subjects: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Psychology. Psychology is **Group 3: individuals and societies**, not a Group 4 science.
Evidence policy: factual claims use official IB sources. The local May 2026 papers are discussed only in a separately labelled structural-observation section and are not curriculum authority. Download metadata is in [`resources/ib/sciences/PROVENANCE.md`](../../resources/ib/sciences/PROVENANCE.md).

## Decision summary

1. A pen-capable response area is strongly useful for the three sciences. Their official assessment language and papers include shown calculations, equations, graph construction, diagram drawing/annotation, chemical structures, and extended responses. No official public source reviewed says a stylus or freehand canvas is itself required.
2. Science Paper 1 is one timed paper delivered as two booklets: **1A selected response** and **1B data/experimental response**, completed together without interruption. The builder may offer 1A-only or 1B-only practice, but it must not mislabel either as a complete official session paper or invent a separate official duration.
3. The closest digital analogue is a **question-linked, bounded response surface**, not an infinite whiteboard. Paper 1A should use selected-response controls with an optional unmarked scratchpad. Paper 1B and Paper 2 need answer boxes that can mix text, equations, ink, graphing, and draw-on-stimulus regions.
4. Psychology should default to typed short and extended responses. Ink is optional scratch support, not an identified assessment requirement. New-course HL Paper 3 needs a persistent resource-booklet/data pane because students interpret graphs and synthesize multiple sources.
5. Presets must begin with **assessment cycle/session → subject → level → paper/component**. This is essential for Psychology, whose legacy course has its last assessment in 2026 while a substantially different course has first assessment in 2027.
6. Do not build an unlicensed IB question library. Use teacher-authored material or a school-authorized workflow; the licensed [IB Questionbank](https://questionbank.ibo.org/) already supplies official questions for all four subjects.

## Reading time: a separate five-minute phase

The current public IB [Access and inclusion policy](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/programmes/dp/pdfs/access-and-inclusion-policy-en.pdf) applies to DP assessments. Section 4.12 states that written/on-screen examinations are preceded by **five minutes of reading time** and that approved additional time does not extend those five minutes. No Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Psychology exception was found in the official guides, papers, schedules, or examiner instructions reviewed.

Therefore, each complete written-paper preset below needs `reading time: 5 min` followed by the listed examination/writing time. A combined science Paper 1 gets one five-minute phase, not another five minutes when moving from 1A to 1B. A component-only teacher mock is custom and should not claim an official reading-time arrangement.

The public policy does **not** establish whether a candidate may annotate, highlight, or otherwise interact during those five minutes. The [2026 DP assessment procedures](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/become-an-ib-school/pdfs/dp-2026-general-regulations.pdf) make the current *Conduct of examinations* booklet the operational authority. Store the interaction rule as `PRC confirmation required`; a conservative mock-mode default is read-only prompts with response entry locked until writing time begins.

The remaining 20% at both levels is a 10-hour, 24-mark scientific investigation. It is a report/submission workflow, not another timed exam canvas.

### Biology response tools

Verified from the guide and official public paper format:

- MCQ selection for 1A;
- data/stimulus viewing with short prose and numeric responses for 1B and Paper 2;
- visible stages of calculations;
- constructing, plotting, interpreting, and sketching graphs, including best-fit relationships and uncertainty/error-bar work where relevant;
- drawing and annotating biological diagrams, images, and micrographs;
- sustained prose for Paper 2 Section B with an enforced answer-choice rule.

Design inference: 1B and Paper 2 should offer per-part response blocks for typed text, numeric answer plus working, inline ink, graph grid, and draw-on-image. The teacher should set the response mode per question, while the selected exam supplies sensible defaults.

### Chemistry response tools

Verified response demands include calculations with working; equations and state symbols; chemical formulae, structures, and representations; annotated diagrams; graph plotting/best-fit work; curve or profile sketches; tables; and short-to-extended prose.

Design inference: Chemistry needs a hybrid answer block: keyboard text plus equation/chemical notation, ink for structures and mechanisms, graph overlays, and visible multi-line calculation space. A generic text box alone is inadequate; an ink-only canvas would also make routine symbols and long explanations needlessly difficult.

### Physics SL Paper 2 mark conflict

Do not silently encode one value as universally official:

- the February 2023 public guide says **55 marks**;
- the official first-examination-2025 specimen question-paper cover says **50 marks**, while the markscheme within that same pack says **55 marks**;
- the newer official public May 2025 paper says **50 marks**;
- the current guide was updated November 2024 and is PRC-only.

For a replica of the retained May 2025 public paper, use 50. For a “current 2026 official” preset, block publication or require coordinator confirmation from the current PRC guide/session materials. Duration and weighting are not in dispute.

### Physics response tools

Verified response demands include multi-stage calculations and “show that” reasoning; equations and units; vector/force/circuit or process diagrams; graph plotting and interpretation; best-fit and maximum/minimum-gradient work; error bars and uncertainty; and explanatory prose.

Design inference: Physics needs numeric answer plus working, an equation-capable editor, a ruler/straight-line ink tool, graph axes with point/line/error-bar primitives, diagram annotation, and ordinary text. Preserve the student’s editable strokes and a deterministic rendered export.

## Psychology: two separate assessment cycles

Psychology belongs under **Individuals and societies (Group 3)**. It should appear in the product’s wider course list, not be misclassified under sciences merely because it uses empirical research and data.

### Psychology input design

Verified facts: the external papers are dominated by short and extended prose; the new Paper 3 requires interpretation of graphs and synthesis across a resource booklet. No official public source reviewed makes freehand drawing part of the answer construct or requires a stylus.

Design inference:

- default to a focused typed-response editor with word count, autosave, and clear section/question association;
- use a fixed source/resource pane for unseen situations, studies, graphs, and the HL Paper 3 booklet;
- offer optional scratch ink that is visibly separate from the assessed answer;
- do not surface science-only graph-construction or equation tools unless a teacher explicitly makes a custom/mock question that needs them.

## What the private May 2026 references add

Read-only local files under `resources/private/past-papers/2026-may/{biology,chemistry,physics}/` were inspected only as structural references. They came from an unofficial mirror, contain scans/watermarks and handwritten candidate work, and are not official curriculum or authenticity evidence. Their hashes and filenames remain solely in the ignored local inventory; no question text or image is reproduced here.

Observed patterns, not IB rules:

- Science 1A pages use selected-response questions, while students often place scratch calculations, equations, or small diagrams near the choices. The assessed response is still the selected option; a digital scratchpad should be optional and marked as unsubmitted/unscored.
- Science 1B pages use bounded per-part answer areas. Biology examples combine prose, calculation, and labelled drawing. Chemistry examples combine data interpretation, graph/trend work, and calculations. Physics examples include plotting or drawing directly on a graph, uncertainty/error-bar work, calculations, and explanation.
- The private references reinforce the 1A/1B split and the need for graph/diagram overlays, but their question counts, page breaks, timezone labels, and available response space are not safe invariants.

## Minimal teacher flow

### 1. Choose the exact exam

Use short dependent selectors:

1. Assessment cycle/session: `Sciences — first assessment 2025 / 2026 session`, `Psychology — last assessment 2026`, or `Psychology — first assessment 2027`.
2. Subject: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Psychology.
3. Level: SL or HL.
4. Paper: only valid complete papers. For sciences, show `Paper 1 (1A + 1B)` and `Paper 2`; offer `1A practice` and `1B practice` under a clearly labelled component-practice option. Psychology Paper 3 is HL-only.

Then show one compact preset summary: five-minute reading phase, writing time, marks/weight, answer-choice rule, calculator status, data/resource booklet, and primary response modes. Official preset values remain locked. “Make this a custom/mock paper” can copy and unlock them without claiming exact IB compliance.

### 2. Add questions with only relevant controls

Common controls:

- number/title and marks;
- prompt or teacher-authored stimulus;
- response area and starting size;
- whether students may add continuation space;
- optional private markscheme/rubric;
- source-rights classification.

Reveal response choices by selected exam:

- Science 1A: `MCQ` plus optional `Unmarked scratchpad`.
- Science 1B: `Short text`, `Numeric + working`, `Graph`, `Draw/annotate`, `Equation/structure`, or a small combination.
- Science Paper 2: the same tools plus `Extended response` and answer-choice grouping where applicable.
- Psychology: `Short typed response`, `Extended typed response`, and `Source-based response`; optional scratch ink stays collapsed.

Calculator and data-booklet settings belong to the exam preset, not each question. Graph, chemical-structure, and drawing controls appear only when the teacher selects a compatible response type.

### 3. Preview and validate

Block or warn on:

- invalid course/level/paper combinations;
- marks not matching a locked preset;
- splitting science Paper 1 into two official timers or inserting an interruption;
- a Biology Paper 2 choice group without the correct number of required responses;
- Chemistry or Physics without the correct clean data booklet;
- Psychology HL Paper 3 without a persistent resource booklet/source pane;
- a graph/draw instruction without an enabled graph or annotation region;
- writing-time entry during the reading phase before current conduct rules are confirmed;
- Physics SL Paper 2 presented as current-official without resolving the 50/55-mark conflict;
- IB-copyrighted content classified as redistributable.

## Recommended response-surface model

Use bounded, question-linked blocks with optional A4-like continuation pages:

- editable vector ink with undo/redo, eraser, palm rejection, ruler/straight line, pan/zoom;
- keyboard text and accessible equation entry alongside—not instead of—ink;
- draw-on-prompt/image and graph-grid overlays;
- graph primitives for points, best-fit lines/curves, error bars, and gradient constructions;
- deterministic PDF export plus retained editable strokes;
- separate submitted-answer and scratch layers, with scratch excluded from marking by default on selected-response items.

An infinite canvas makes page association, printing, exam review, and question-level marking harder. It also diverges from the official answer-box/booklet model without a demonstrated assessment benefit.

## Rights boundary

The retained official May 2025 science papers are public URLs but carry a notice that expressly prohibits third-party/app-developer reuse. They are ignored local references, not application assets. The private 2026 references have an even stronger provenance restriction because they came from an unauthorized mirror.

Store a rights classification on every imported source or question:

- `teacher-authored`;
- `school-authorized/licensed`;
- `official public reference — local only`;
- `private/unverified reference — local only`;
- `unknown — sharing and publication blocked`.

The Paper Builder should generate structure and response space around teacher-authored or licensed material; it should not reproduce IB questions, stationery, diagrams, or markschemes.
