# IB DP language assessment and Paper Builder findings

Research snapshot: **2026-09-01**
Scope: English, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish Language A; English B and Spanish B.
Evidence policy: factual claims use official IB sources. Local May 2026 files are discussed only as private structural observations and are not curriculum authority. Download details are in [`resources/ib/languages/PROVENANCE.md`](../../resources/ib/languages/PROVENANCE.md).

## Decision summary

1. Language papers should default to focused typed prose or short-answer fields beside persistent, zoomable source material. A freehand canvas is optional scratch support, not an identified IB requirement.
2. Presets must start with `assessment session/cycle → course → level → paper/component`. The current Language A instructions refer to first-assessment-2026 guides, while Language B remains on its first-assessment-2020 cycle; the announced Language B revision is first assessed in 2029.
3. Language B listening needs three separately scoped audio resources and an explicit playback sequence. A generic two-play button is useful practice but does not reproduce the verified specimen cadence by itself.
4. Korean and Japanese delivery needs IME, font, wrapping, autosave, and PDF-rendering checks before classroom use. This is a product requirement inferred from the writing systems, not a special IB input rule.
5. Public specimens and guides remain copyrighted. Use teacher-authored or school-authorized content in DigitalDP and obtain current protected materials through the PRC, IB Questionbank, or the school coordinator.

## Current 2026 Language A presets

The same external paper structure applies to the researched Language A instances. The official [2026 subject list](../../resources/ib/languages/all-dp-subjects-list-2026.pdf) verifies that the named language/course variants exist; it does not establish which ones this school actually offers.

| Preset | Writing time | Required response | DigitalDP default |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Literature or Language & Literature SL Paper 1 | 75 min | Choose one of two unseen texts; write one guided analysis | Two source-linked essay cards with paper-level choose-one behaviour |
| Literature or Language & Literature HL Paper 1 | 135 min | Analyse both unseen texts separately | Two required source-linked essay cards |
| Literature or Language & Literature SL/HL Paper 2 | 105 min | Choose one of four prompts; write one closed-book comparative essay using two studied works | Four essay cards with paper-level choose-one behaviour |

Each complete written paper above also receives a separate **five-minute reading phase** under the current public [Access and inclusion policy](../../resources/ib/policies/access-and-inclusion-policy-current-2026.pdf). Additional-time percentages do not extend those five minutes. The public policy does not say whether annotation or highlighting is permitted then; keep response and annotation tools locked as the conservative mock default and confirm the live rule in the PRC conduct instructions.

### Coursework and oral tasks

- The Language A higher level essay is externally assessed coursework of **1,200–1,500 words**, not a timed paper.
- The Language A individual oral is a **15-minute recorded assessment**, not a paper. Literature uses extracts from two works, one translated and one originally written in the course language, focused through a global issue. Language & Literature uses a literary extract and a non-literary body-of-work extract through a global issue.
- No fixed separate Language A oral preparation duration was verified in the current public sources reviewed. Do not invent one; confirm it in the current guide/PRC materials.

These tasks need document-submission or recording workflows rather than the present timed Paper Builder.

## Current 2026 Language B presets

| Preset | Writing/exam time | Required response | DigitalDP default |
|---|---:|---|---|
| SL Paper 1 — productive skills | 75 min | Choose one of three tasks and an appropriate listed text type; 250–400 words | Three rich-text essay cards, choose one |
| HL Paper 1 — productive skills | 90 min | Choose one of three tasks and an appropriate listed text type; 450–600 words | Three rich-text essay cards, choose one |
| SL/HL Paper 2 — reading | 60 min | Answer all questions linked to three written texts | Mixed short and selected responses scoped to each text |
| SL Paper 2 — listening | 45 min | Answer all questions linked to three audio texts | Three scoped audio resources and mixed short/selected responses |
| HL Paper 2 — listening | 60 min | Same structure as SL | Same as SL |

Paper 1 and the reading component each receive the separate general five-minute reading phase described above. Do **not** automatically add that general phase to listening: the reviewed public language-specific specimen establishes an internal per-text cadence, while whether an additional general pre-start period applies remains unverified publicly.

### Listening cadence

The official public [English B specimen pack](../../resources/ib/languages/english-b-hl-sl-specimen-papers-and-markschemes-first-assessment-2020.pdf) specifies, for each of three audio texts:

1. four minutes to read the relevant questions;
2. the first play, with answering permitted at any time;
3. a two-minute pause;
4. the second play.

Current 2026 instructions confirm three audio texts but do not restate the full playback script. Make the cadence visible and coordinator-confirmable. The current DigitalDP player records a play limit but does not schedule previews or pauses; exact-cadence practice therefore needs proctor-managed playback or preassembled sequence assets and must be labelled as an approximation until scheduled audio blocks are implemented.

### Language B individual oral

- SL: 15 minutes preparation, then a 15-minute oral based on one of two visual stimuli.
- HL: 20 minutes preparation, then a 15-minute oral based on one of two literary extracts.
- Both are three-part recorded oral tasks. They need a dedicated preparation/recording workflow, not the written-paper timer.

## Input and media requirements

### Verified assessment needs

- Persistent, zoomable layout-preserving text/image sources for Language A Paper 1.
- Rich text with paragraphing, emphasis, lists, tables where appropriate, live word count, autosave, and reliable recovery for extended writing.
- Question-scoped written text and audio resources for Language B Paper 2.
- Multiple audio uploads, ordered by text, with an explicit play count and playback history.
- A deterministic print/PDF record containing every teacher-entered prompt and the student's response; source PDFs/audio may remain companion resources, while prompt images should print inline.

### Product inferences

- Default to typed answers. Optional ink may help planning or annotation but should not replace accessible text input.
- For Korean and Japanese, test IME composition events, cursor movement, undo/redo, vertical and horizontal punctuation behaviour where relevant, font fallback, line breaking, local draft recovery during composition, and final PDF glyph embedding.
- Images need teacher-authored descriptions/captions. Audio needs a readable transcript or approved accessibility alternative where required by a student's arrangements.
- Avoid copied IB branding. Use an original, restrained examination-paper layout with clear candidate metadata, instructions, numbered questions, response areas, and page numbers.

## Minimal teacher flow

1. Choose the assessment session, exact course, level, and paper/component.
2. Show a compact preset summary: separate reading phase when applicable, writing time, number of source/audio texts, answer-choice rule, and word range.
3. Add each source once, then add its questions and response areas. Keep question-specific media scoped to that question.
4. For listening, require audio and show the play limit/cadence before publication.
5. Preview the student view and printable candidate record. Warn if the selected language has not passed IME/font/PDF QA.
6. If timing or core instructions are edited, save the result as a custom mock rather than retaining an official-looking session profile.
