# Teacher review — Sample Candidate L1

This is a worked human-marking example, not an automatic grade boundary.

| Question | Award | Available | Reason |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | Correct main purpose. |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | Correct venue. |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | Correct motivation. |
| 4 | 1 | 2 | The retired engineer is valid; a carpenter is not mentioned. |
| 5 | 1 | 2 | The total of 68 is correct, but 41—not 40—were repaired. |
| 6 | 2 | 3 | The mobile cart and minibus transport are identified; secure storage is omitted. |
| **Total** | **7** | **10** |  |

The submission gives a teacher useful evidence of partial listening: the candidate generally understood purpose and sequence but lost precision when several details appeared together. A sensible follow-up is a short practice in recording paired numbers and list items. The printed candidate record should show the audio label and play limit, but it must not reveal this transcript or assessment.
