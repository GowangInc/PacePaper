The answer is deliberately submitted through the typed alternative with two empty ink pages. That makes an important output case visible: a teacher must be able to distinguish “no drawing” from “blank response,” read the typed working, and still identify that the requested graph was omitted. The notepad is private working and should not appear on the printed candidate record.
